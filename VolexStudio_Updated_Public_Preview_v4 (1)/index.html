WHO WE ARE

A creative corner
near Tezpur AFS.

VolexStudio is an emerging art and design studio based around Air Force Station Tezpur. We are bringing artists and creative thinkers together to build a thoughtful portfolio of original illustrations, character concepts, portraits, colour studies and visual design.

Right now, the site is a showcase—not a live shop. We are not taking direct sales yet. You can contact us to ask about a piece, talk through an idea, or learn what the studio is developing.
